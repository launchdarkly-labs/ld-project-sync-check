Are your projects in sync?

Compares two LaunchDarkly projects field by field, as your SDKs would see them. Tokens are sent only to LaunchDarkly and are never stored unless you ask.

This page runs entirely in your browser: tokens go from here straight to LaunchDarkly. Use reader tokens, and remove them when you are done. To keep tokens off the browser, run the command-line version instead.

A report JSON from this tool, for example one your migration team sent you. Nothing is fetched.
